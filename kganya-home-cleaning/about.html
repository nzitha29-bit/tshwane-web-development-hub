About Kganya Home Cleaning Services

Built on trust, referrals and a commitment to spotless homes.

Our history

Kganya Home Cleaning Services was founded in 2019 by Naledi Dube, a Pretoria-based entrepreneur who saw a growing need for reliable, professional domestic cleaning in Gauteng. What began as one team of three staff and eight households has grown, through word-of-mouth referrals, into a company serving over 150 residential and commercial clients across Pretoria East, Centurion and Midrand.

Today, twelve full-time cleaners and two administrative staff keep the business running, and this website marks the next step in reaching even more households across the region.

What we stand for

To deliver trustworthy, affordable and high-quality cleaning solutions to homes and businesses across Gauteng, while creating dignified employment opportunities for local communities.

To be the most recognised and trusted residential cleaning brand in the Pretoria and Centurion metropolitan area by 2028.

  • Trust: the same vetted team visits your home whenever possible.
  • Reliability: we arrive on time and finish what we promised.
  • Dignified work: our cleaners are permanent staff with fair pay and training.
  • Care: we treat your home and belongings as if they were our own.
