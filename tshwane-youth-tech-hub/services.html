Our programmes

All programmes are free for beneficiaries and run in partnership with local venues in Soshanguve and Atteridgeville.

Coding bootcamps

Intro to Web Development

An 8-week introduction to HTML, CSS and JavaScript for learners aged 16–25. No prior experience required.

Intro to Python

A 6-week programme covering programming fundamentals using Python, for learners who have completed the digital literacy workshop.

Digital literacy workshops

Weekend workshops covering computer basics, internet safety, email and essential office software — designed for learners with little to no prior computer exposure.

Career readiness

CV writing, interview practice, and guidance on bursaries, learnerships and entry-level tech roles, delivered alongside our bootcamp graduates.
