Our programmes

All programmes are free for learners aged 16–25 and run at venues in Soshanguve, Atteridgeville and Mamelodi.

What we offer

Coding bootcamps

Intro to Web Development is an 8-week introduction to HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no prior experience required.

Intro to Python is a 6-week programme in programming fundamentals for learners who have completed the digital literacy workshop.

Digital literacy workshops

Weekend workshops covering computer basics, internet safety, email and essential office software, designed for learners with little to no prior computer experience.

Career readiness

CV writing, interview practice, and guidance on bursaries, learnerships and entry-level tech roles, delivered alongside our bootcamp graduates.
